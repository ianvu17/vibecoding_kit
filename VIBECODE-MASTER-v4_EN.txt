## CONTEXT QUESTIONS (Adjust according to project type)

### Universal Questions (Ask all types):

```
1. PRODUCTS/SERVICES:
   Briefly describe what you are doing?
   _______________________________________________

2. TARGET CUSTOMERS:
   Who will use it? (age, occupation, pain point)
   _______________________________________________

3. BRAND:
   □ Already have brand guidelines → Share
   □ Not yet → I will suggest

4. DIFFERENCES:
   What's different from the vision I proposed?
   _______________________________________________
```

### Type-Specific Questions:

```
FOR LANDING PAGE:
• Main goal: Collect leads? Sell? Booking?
• Are there any special offers/promotions?

FOR SAAS APP:
• What are the 3 most important features?
• User roles: Only 1 or many types?

FOR DASHBOARD:
• Which data need to be visualized?
• Who will see this dashboard? (execs, teams, clients)

FOR BLOG/DOCS:
• Publishing frequency?
• Need complex categories/tags?

FOR PORTFOLIO:
• Occupation/field?
• Are there detailed case studies or just showcases?
```
